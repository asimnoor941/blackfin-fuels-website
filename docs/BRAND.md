# Brand reference

Derived from the live site and logo. There is no formal brand guide.

## Logo

A line-drawn dark navy fin above a light blue wave, with "BLACKFIN" in navy, "FUELS" in light blue and "MARINE FUEL DELIVERY" beneath. Only a raster PNG exists (`reference/original-site/media/2026/07/BF-logo-RGB-01-7a391c13-1920w.png`). A vector version is needed; if the owner cannot supply one, redraw it as SVG.

The fin's concentric line work is the most distinctive visual in the brand and should drive the motion language: contour lines, wave lines, sonar-style rings.

Proposed direction for the rebuild: dark-first. Deep navy to near-black as the canvas, `#5FAFFF` as the single accent, white type, and brushed aluminium from the tankers as the material reference for 3D surfaces.

## Voice

Plain, confident, operational. Short declarative lines: "Skip the marina stop." "No marina lines. No fuel docks." "Professional logistics, not guesswork." It sells reliability and professionalism, not luxury. The executive feel should come from restraint and precision, not from luxury-yacht clichés.

Tagline on the trucks: **Fuel Delivered to Your Dock.**
Founder line: **Built by boaters, for boaters.**
